RepLock iOS — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026

Scope and operator

This policy covers RepLock for iPhone and iPad (com.sakovkid.replock.ios), provided by SakovKid. Contact: sakovkid@gmail.com. RepLock is a personal screen-time and exercise utility. It has no user accounts, ads, in-app purchases, HealthKit integration or social features in this version.

Camera and exercise recognition

When you start a workout, RepLock uses the camera and Google ML Kit to recognize body movements on your device. RepLock does not record, store or upload workout video or pose landmarks. No microphone access is requested. The app counts repetitions or plank time locally. ML Kit may separately send technical diagnostics described below; turning off Firebase analytics does not disable ML Kit diagnostics.

Screen Time and local data

With your permission, Apple Family Controls lets you select apps, categories or websites to protect. RepLock uses Apple-provided selection tokens and local usage reports to manage earned access. It does not read messages, passwords or screen content. Selections, workout history, rewards, access balance and preferences are stored in an App Group on the device and are not sent to RepLock analytics. RepLock has no app-operated cloud sync. Apple backups or device transfers may contain app data under your system settings.

Technical SDK diagnostics

Google ML Kit and its dependencies may send installation identifiers, device and app information, API configuration, performance metrics and diagnostic/error information to Google for SDK functionality and analytics. These diagnostics are independent of the optional Firebase choices. See the linked ML Kit disclosure and terms for details. Camera inputs and pose outputs remain local.

Optional analytics and crash reports

If you explicitly allow analytics, Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics process app interactions, completed exercise types, installation-level identifiers, approximate region inferred by Google from network information, and crash/diagnostic information. These help us understand usage and fix faults. RepLock does not send repetition counts, workout duration, workout history, balances, protected-app selections or Screen Time usage. Advertising storage, ad personalization and IDFV collection are disabled by the app. RepLock does not use IDFA or track you across other companies’ apps or websites for advertising.

Optional reminders and RepLock news

Daily reminders are scheduled locally if you allow them. If you separately enable RepLock news, Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging process push/installation tokens and device/app metadata to deliver RepLock updates. News in this release is limited to RepLock, with no promotion of other apps. News and analytics choices are separate. Notifications can also be disabled in iOS Settings.

Your choices, retention and deletion

Analytics and news are optional and off until you choose them. You can change them in RepLock Settings. Disabling analytics stops collection through the app, resets local Analytics data and deletes unsent Crashlytics reports; disabling news requests deletion of the FCM token and stops news registration. These actions do not automatically erase data already processed by Google. Google retention and deletion depend on the applicable service and its settings; consult Firebase’s privacy information below or contact us about a data request. Local workout data can be cleared in RepLock Settings. Uninstalling the app removes its installed local data, subject to any Apple backup or device-transfer copy you control. We do not maintain an additional RepLock server copy of workouts or camera data.

Children and privacy requests

RepLock is not offered in Apple’s Kids category and does not request birth dates. Screen Time authorization is governed by Apple and the device’s account settings. If you believe a child’s personal information was provided to us, or want to request access, correction or deletion of personal data where applicable, email sakovkid@gmail.com. RepLock has no login, so an email address alone may not identify installation-level Google data. Do not send camera recordings, Screen Time tokens or passwords with a support request.

Processing, security and changes

Google and Apple may process technical data in other countries under their applicable policies. We limit app telemetry to the purposes described here, but cannot guarantee absolute security of any storage or transmission. If practices change, we will update this page and its effective date. This policy concerns the iOS app; Android and other SakovKid products may have separate policies.
