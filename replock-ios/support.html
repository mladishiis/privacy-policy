RepLock iOS — Support

Effective date: 3 November 2026

What RepLock does

Choose the apps you want a break from, complete a short camera-counted exercise, and use earned time to access those apps. One coin corresponds to one minute of access. RepLock requires iOS/iPadOS 17 or later. The app does not provide medical advice; choose exercises and a safe space appropriate for you.

Set up app protection

In RepLock Settings, choose apps and allow Apple Screen Time access. Select at least one app, return to RepLock and ensure protection is active. Earn access through a workout. Usage accounting and shield updates depend on Apple Screen Time reports and may not update every second.

Camera and counting

Allow camera access, place the device steadily and keep the required body parts visible. Follow the exercise setup hints. You can use either camera. RepLock pauses training when it goes into the background or the capture session is interrupted. Return to the workout and resume it; check camera access in iOS Settings if necessary. No video is saved. The iPad layout has been tested in simulators; physical iPad camera and Screen Time behavior have not been verified by the developer.

Privacy, reminders and data

Analytics, daily reminders and news can be changed separately in Settings. The privacy section links to the app policy and SDK information. Workout data can be cleared in Settings; this does not delete data previously processed by Google services.

Contact support

Email sakovkid@gmail.com. Include RepLock’s version, device model, iOS/iPadOS version, what happened and steps to reproduce. Screenshots are optional; remove personal information first. Do not send camera recordings, passwords, Apple Account credentials or protected-app tokens.
